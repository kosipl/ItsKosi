About
Kosi

Producing since 2017 and DJing since 2022, Kosi works open format — Hip-Hop, R&B, and Dancehall held together by reading the room rather than a fixed setlist.

Seattle is home base, with regular travel for dates across the U.S. and abroad. The rooms range from a 200-capacity club to a 2,000-plus outdoor crowd, with every set shaped to the room, the audience, and the moment.
